## Brand & Style

The brand personality centers on master craftsmanship, dependable execution, and architectural precision. Designed for homeowners, interior designers, commercial property managers, and general contractors, the interface inspires confidence through immaculate attention to detail, clarity of process, and enduring quality.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate Clarity** with **Refined Industrial Craftsmanship**. It avoids gimmicks or heavy-handed painterly textures, favoring sharp compositional clarity, balanced white space, purposeful micro-interactions, and premium tactile cues. Crisp card boundaries, subtle structural borders, and purposeful contrast communicate reliability, clean job sites, and premier architectural finish work.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative architectural blues with warm gold and amber tones that evoke high-grade materials, warmth, and certified expertise.

- **Primary (`#1D4ED8` / deep variant `#1E40AF`):** Royal cobalt blue anchors headers, primary action buttons, focused states, and structural brand moments. It carries the weight of institutional authority and reliable project execution.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` / deep shade `#D97706`):** Warm amber gold accents key value drivers, certifications, ratings, and conversion targets such as "Request Estimate" actions.
- **Tertiary (`#FBBF24`):** Vibrant golden yellow applied sparingly for attention-grabbing badges, star ratings, highlighting warranties, and progress tracking markers.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate provides optimal typographic readability and contrast for high-priority headings, primary copy, and solid dark footers or banners.
- **Neutral Light & Canvas (`#F8FAFC` & `#FFFFFF`):** High-luminance backgrounds maintain an airy, clean-slate environment reminiscent of a freshly primed interior wall. Subtle slate borders (`#E2E8F0`) frame interactive surfaces.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to project contemporary precision, approachability, and structural neatness. 

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in bold (`700`) and extra-bold (`800`) weights with tight line heights and negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` on `headline-xl`) to deliver punchy, architectural strength.
- **Body Text:** Scaled to comfortable line heights (`1.5` to `1.55`) using regular (`400`) and medium (`500`) weights for rapid scanning of quotes, surface prep checklists, and scope descriptions.
- **Labels & Metrics:** Styled with medium to bold weights (`600`–`700`), often featuring slight positive letter-spacing (`+0.04em` on `label-sm`) for uppercase metadata tags, paint finishes, and project stages.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements a structured 12-column fluid grid system on desktop that snaps neatly to content boundaries, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet, and a single or 2-column stacked layout on mobile viewports.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (Margins: `1.25rem`, Gutters: `1rem`)
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (Margins: `2rem`, Gutters: `1.25rem`)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (Max layout container: `1280px`, Margins: `3rem`, Gutters: `1.5rem`)
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Based on an 8pt modular scale. Dense informational panels use `space-sm` (`8px`) and `space-md` (`16px`) gaps, while quote calculators, service cards, and portfolio comparisons use generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to establish an unhurried, luxury contractor presentation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses flat, high-definition surfaces supported by crisp low-contrast borders and subtle ambient drops:

- **Surface Strategy:** The primary canvas sits on light slate (`#F8FAFC`). Cards, comparison tables, and quote panels are pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to pop with clean contrast.
- **Borders & Outlines:** Every card and modal is enclosed with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0` or `#CBD5E1`) creating razor-sharp separation without visual clutter.
- **Shadow Profile:** Shadows are diffused, tinted with deep navy-slate rather than pure black:
  - *Base Cards / Containers:* `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`
  - *Floating / Hover Elements:* `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
  - *Modals & Estimator Overlays:* `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`
- **Interactive Depth:** On hover, primary interactive cards elevate subtly by moving `-2px` along the Y-axis accompanied by an increased amber or royal blue border glow.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances structural authority with user-friendly ergonomics. Surfaces feature a balanced `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius, ensuring forms, cards, and interactive controls feel crafted rather than utilitarian.

- **Standard Elements (inputs, buttons, cards):** `0.5rem` (`8px`)
- **Elevated Modals & Large Panels:** `1rem` (`16px`)
- **Status Tags, Color Swatches, & Micro Chips:** `9999px` (Pill-shaped) for quick visual scanning.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Request Quote", "Book Inspection"):** Solid Royal Cobalt (`#1D4ED8`) background, white text, semi-bold weight, `0.5rem` border-radius, generous horizontal padding (`1.5rem`), and vertical padding (`0.75rem`). Hover state darkens to `#1E40AF` with a soft blue ambient halo.
- **Secondary / Action ("Schedule Call"):** Warm Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`) background with slate text (`#0F172A`) or deep white-contrasting tone for high-conversion prompts.
- **Outline / Ghost:** 1px border in `#CBD5E1` over white, text in `#0F172A`, transitions to `#F1F5F9` background on hover.

### Cards & Service Containers
- **Construction:** Crisp pure white surfaces (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), `0.5rem` radius, and `1.5rem` to `2rem` internal padding.
- **Interactive Variants:** Hovering triggers a subtle lift and shifts the border tint toward `#1D4ED8`. Featured warranty or flagship service tiers incorporate a top border accent bar (4px height) in `#F59E0B`.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Clean white background, 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), `0.5rem` corner radius, `0.75rem 1rem` padding.
- **Active / Focus:** Border transitions to `#1D4ED8` with a 3px matching outer ring at 15% opacity (`rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.15)`).
- **Labels:** Positioned atop inputs in `label-md` using slate neutral (`#0F172A`), with helper text in muted slate (`#64748B`).

### Chips & Finish Badges
- **Status & Finish Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- **Variants:** "Interior Master", "Exterior Commercial", or "Eggshell / Satin" chips use light tinted backgrounds (`#EFF6FF` for blue, `#FEF3C7` for gold) paired with dark saturated text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Control Form:** Custom 18px rounded squares (checkbox) or circles (radio) with 1.5px border (`#94A3B8`).
- **Checked State:** Fill transitions immediately to `#1D4ED8` with a crisp white checkmark icon or center dot.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Color Swatch Selector:** Square swatches (`48x48px`) with rounded corners, displaying paint finish names, LRV values, and sheen level badges. Active swatch displays an amber gold selection ring.
- **Before / After Comparison Slider:** Clean divider line with amber handle grip and high-contrast pill labels anchored at opposite corners.
- **Project Progress Tracker:** Linear step sequence connecting completed phases (Prep, Prime, Coat 1, Coat 2, Final Walkthrough) via royal cobalt connectors and numbered nodes.
