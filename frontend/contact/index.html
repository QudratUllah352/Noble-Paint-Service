Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our estimates, scheduling, and paint materials.

A master estimator visits your location with precision laser measures and moisture scanners. We inspect drywall condition, analyze previous coat sheens, evaluate natural lighting angles, and provide architectural fan decks (Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, Farrow & Ball). Within 24 hours, you receive a digital, transparent line-item estimate detailing prep, primer, coats, and duration.
We honor comparable estimates from reputable, fully licensed and insured painting contractors. The proposal must outline identical surface preparation (caulking, skimming, sanding) and top-tier paint formulas (such as Benjamin Moore Regal Select or Aura). Simply present their written quotation prior to signing.
